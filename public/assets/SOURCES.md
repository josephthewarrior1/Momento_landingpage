# Momento landing imagery

These six photos are local WebP exports of the invitation studio's own AI-generated fictional examples. The hero images retain their original 1024 px width; the other cards are resized to 800 px width. They are editable example photography, not customer portraits or testimonials. No competitor photography, logos, partner badges, or product screenshots are used.
