# Atelier assets

Atelier's five original illustrations were generated for this project, following
the approved Atelier concept board. The villa, garden estate, dining table and
transparent botanical branch and hand-holding sketch are illustration assets, not photographs. They are
stored locally as optimized WebP/PNG files. Typography uses locally bundled EB
Garamond and Pinyon Script, distributed under the SIL Open Font License.

The photographs depict real adults and are used as fictional editable examples.
All four are by **Micah & Sammie Chaffin** (https://thechaffins.co/) on Unsplash,
downloaded October 18, 2026. Source pages identify them as free under the Unsplash
License: https://unsplash.com/license . No Unsplash+ images are included.

The first image supplies two independently editable portrait crops of the same
couple. The remaining images form a consistent Venice wedding series. No photos
from Nathalie, Vow Paper, or any other Momento template are reused. Fictional
names, example venue information, and example bank details do not identify the
people in the photographs.

The envelope, map pin, heart, ribbon, string lights, dashed garden path, and paper
grain are native SVG/CSS components. Garden labels and event details remain live
editable text rather than being baked into the generated illustrations.
